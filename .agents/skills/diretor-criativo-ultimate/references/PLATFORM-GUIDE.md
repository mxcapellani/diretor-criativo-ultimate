# Platform Guide — Diretor Criativo Ultimate

Version: 1.0.0

## Purpose

This guide defines creative adaptation profiles for supported visual generation tools.

It supplements SKILL.md.

It does not claim access to vendor internals, private model documentation, or verified API parameters.

All recommendations are textual direction heuristics.

## 1. Kling 3.0

### Creative emphasis

Motion architecture and spatial continuity.

### Prioritize

- Stable subject identity.
- Clearly motivated movement.
- Defined camera direction.
- Believable acceleration and deceleration.
- Physical interactions.
- Coherent initial and final states.
- Consistent spatial relationships.

### Construction approach

Describe the environment, place the subject, establish the initial frame, introduce the action, describe the camera response, and end in a coherent final composition.

### Useful shot choices

- Dolly In
- Dolly Out
- Truck Left
- Truck Right
- Orbit/Arc
- FPV Drone
- Pull Back Reveal

### Common failure risks

- Too many simultaneous camera movements.
- Conflicting subject trajectories.
- Ambiguous beginnings or endings.
- Impossible spatial transitions.
- Changes in wardrobe or object identity.

### Quality question

Can the action and camera trajectory be clearly imagined as one physically continuous sequence?

## 2. Seedance 2.0

### Creative emphasis

Commercial beauty, fluid movement, and atmospheric integration.

### Prioritize

- Smooth movement.
- Graceful visual rhythm.
- Air, water, fabric, and particle response.
- Lighting transitions.
- Environmental interaction.
- Spatial depth.
- Premium finish appropriate to the brand.

### Useful shot choices

- Crane/Jib/Boom
- Dolly In
- Orbit/Arc
- Tilt Up
- Rack Focus
- Pull Back Reveal

### Common failure risks

- Excessive atmosphere hiding the subject.
- Motion with no creative purpose.
- Too many unrelated effects.
- Changes in product appearance.
- Visually dense scenes with no clear hierarchy.

### Quality question

Does the atmosphere deepen the commercial idea rather than compete with it?

## 3. Higgsfield

### Creative emphasis

Human emotion, performance, and visual storytelling.

### Prioritize

- Facial micro-expression.
- Gaze direction.
- Posture and gesture.
- Emotional progression.
- Camera proximity.
- Human-scale environmental detail.
- Meaningful reactions.

### Useful shot choices

- Medium Shot
- Medium Close-Up
- Close-Up
- Extreme Close-Up
- Over-the-Shoulder
- Dolly In
- Rack Focus

### Common failure risks

- Emotion expressed only as abstract adjectives.
- Generic expressions.
- Unmotivated camera movement.
- Inconsistent eye direction.
- Excessive gestures that weaken realism.

### Quality question

Could an actor interpret the described performance as a meaningful emotional beat?

## 4. Nano Banana Pro

### Creative emphasis

Photographic authenticity and material realism.

### Prioritize

- Product or subject appearance.
- Complete composition.
- Surface response to light.
- Shadow consistency.
- Material texture.
- Reflective behavior.
- Editorial color relationships.
- Controlled depth.

### Useful shot choices

- Static
- Full Shot
- Medium Shot
- Close-Up
- Extreme Close-Up
- Macro Shot
- Eye-Level
- Low Angle

### Common failure risks

- Excessive action verbs in a static prompt.
- Contradictory lighting.
- Unrealistic reflections.
- Inconsistent scale.
- Vague material descriptions.
- Unnecessary rendering terminology.

### Quality question

Could a professional photographer plausibly construct this scene using physical subjects and real lighting?

## 5. Universal mode

Use when no platform is specified.

### Prioritize

- Clear subject.
- Professional framing.
- Coherent camera description.
- Motivated light.
- Authentic materials.
- Atmospheric depth.
- Subject interaction.
- A readable visual focal point.

Avoid platform-specific claims.

## 7. Multi-platform translation

When converting prompts:

1. Preserve the principal subject.
2. Preserve the user's narrative and brand constraints.
3. Preserve essential environmental features.
4. Preserve the original emotional purpose.
5. Change only the platform-sensitive direction.

### From still to motion

Add:

- Initial state.
- Specific primary action.
- Camera trajectory.
- Physical movement response.
- Final state.

Do not automatically invent a complex narrative.

### From motion to still

Choose the most visually expressive decisive moment.

Remove unnecessary temporal sequences.

Retain material, atmosphere, emotion, and framing.

### From universal to specialized

Emphasize the platform-specific attributes without adding unrelated effects.

## 8. Default parameter guide

| Use case | Aspect ratio | Duration | Lens feel |
|---|---|---|---|
| General cinematic video | 16:9 | 8 seconds | 35mm |
| Vertical campaign | 9:16 | 6–10 seconds | 35mm |
| Narrative portrait video | 16:9 | 8 seconds | 50mm |
| Editorial portrait still | 4:5 | N/A | 85mm |
| Product still | 1:1 or 4:5 | N/A | 50mm or macro |
| Epic landscape film | 2.39:1 | 8–10 seconds | 24mm |

These values are suggested creative defaults.

Explicit user settings always take priority.

## 9. Physical continuity checklist

Check that:

- Objects do not change shape without reason.
- Light direction remains credible.
- Reflections correspond to the scene.
- Camera travel respects physical space.
- Subject movement and camera movement are distinguishable.
- Atmosphere responds plausibly.
- Moving fabric reacts to a consistent force.
- Focus behavior supports the narrative.
- Product identity remains stable.
- End-state composition follows from prior motion.

## 10. Prompt language quality

Use:

- Concrete subjects.
- Observable actions.
- Motivated lighting.
- Spatial relationships.
- Photographic texture.
- Precise cinematic shot names.
- Coherent temporal sequencing.

Avoid:

- Endless adjective stacking.
- Empty quality labels.
- Contradictory optics.
- Arbitrary camera choreography.
- Unsupported technical guarantees.
- Engine and resolution buzzwords.
- Unexplained identity changes.
- Impossibly simultaneous actions.

## 11. Known limitations

Tool capabilities and version-specific behaviors may evolve.

This guide cannot guarantee:

- Platform parameter support.
- Model adherence.
- Precise motion accuracy.
- Exact composition.
- Face or character continuity.
- Text and logo fidelity.
- Frame-perfect camera trajectories.

Treat generated results as material for creative iteration.
